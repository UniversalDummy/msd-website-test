About MrScriptDev

Who Am I...?
Hi! I'm MrScriptDev, a systems level programmer. I love to write code in my free time, whether it be a simple program, a website, a script, and beyond!
What Is A Systems Level Programmer...?
A systems level programmer is someone who writes programs that interact closely or directly with computer hardware. Systems level programmers are the ones responsible for developing operating systems, embedded systems, firmware, etc.
My Coding Journey...
I started my coding journey on a website called Scratch, dragging simple blocks onto a workspace to make items and objects move around at the press of a button. Eventually, I transitioned to Godot, a free and open source game engine, for a short period of time. It was my first experience with writing out code rather than dragging it. However, I eventually moved away from Godot and began teaching myself the basics of C++, where I made simple and fun projects. Shortly after learning the basics, I competed in a programming competition under an organization called SkillsUSA, where I won a gold medal in the Locals competition. I then began my signature project, called The Silicon Grind, which would become the most valuable project I had ever worked on.
Experience...
At 12 years of age is when I began coding in Scratch. I advanced enough to be writing simple C++ by 14 years old, and eventually in C by 15 years old and raw x86 binary by 16 years old.
The Silicon Grind...
At 15 years old, I started a project that quickly became the largest project I had ever worked on, called The Silicon Grind. I studied CPU architecture independently and developed my own open source toolchain involving a very small language used to hand-encode raw bits efficiently and orderly, allowing me to write bootable x86 programs in machine code by hand.
