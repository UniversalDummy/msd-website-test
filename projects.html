The Silicon Grind
The Silicon Grind is MrScriptDev's most valuable, most important, most successfull, and largest project he has ever made. Code named "Project Summer 2025" and "Project Summer 2026," MrScriptDev continues to work on the project actively.

This MSD project involves low level programming. Utilizing a custom toolchain developed in C, x86 CPU instructions are hand-encoded bit by bit and burned to be bootable on a disk.
